Background of the Business

Amma Unavagam is a restaurant that specialises in serving Kothu Roti, a popular South Indian and Sri Lankan street food. The idea behind starting this business is to provide customers with delicious, freshly made, and affordable traditional food in a welcoming environment. Many people enjoy authentic homemade flavours, and Amma Unavagam aims to bring the taste of traditional family cooking to the local community.

What is Kothu?

Kothu Roti (or Kothu Parotta) is a popular dish made by chopping flatbread (parotta or roti) into small pieces and stir-frying it with vegetables, eggs, meat, and a blend of spices on a large hot griddle. The ingredients are mixed together while being finely chopped, creating a flavourful and satisfying meal. It is well known for its delicious taste, unique preparation method, and variety of options, including chicken, beef, egg, and vegetarian versions.

Why I Am Starting This Business

I am starting Amma Unavagam because I want to share the rich flavours of South Indian and Sri Lankan cuisine with my community. Kothu is a popular and affordable dish that appeals to a wide range of customers. My goal is to provide high-quality food, excellent customer service, and an authentic dining experience while promoting traditional culture and cuisine.

Mission Statement:

"To serve authentic, tasty, and affordable Kothu dishes while creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere for all customers."
