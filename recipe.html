Ingredients for Mutton Kothu

  • Parotta (chopped)
  • Mutton (cooked and shredded or diced)
  • Eggs
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Green chillies
  • Curry leaves
  • Ginger and garlic
  • Chilli powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Coriander powder
  • Garam masala or kothu masala
  • Oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves

Cooking Instructions

  1. Heat oil on a large griddle or frying pan.
  2. Sauté the onions, green chillies, curry leaves, ginger, and garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook until softened.
  4. Stir in the chilli powder, turmeric powder, coriander powder, and garam masala.
  5. Add the cooked mutton and mix well with the spices.
  6. Crack the eggs into the pan and scramble them with the mixture.
  7. Add the chopped parotta and toss everything together.
  8. Finely chop and stir-fry the mixture until the flavours are fully combined.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
  10. Serve hot and enjoy your authentic Mutton Kothu.
